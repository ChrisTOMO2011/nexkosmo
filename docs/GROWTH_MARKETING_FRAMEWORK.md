# Nexkosmo Growth & Marketing Framework

Status: APPROVED OPERATING FRAMEWORK
Authority: Director
Scope: Positioning, audience development, acquisition, activation, retention, referral, creator success, launch planning, measurement, and growth experiments.

## Purpose

Nexkosmo marketing must be built as deliberately as Nexkosmo engineering. The goal is not to maximize impressions, clicks, messages, or user dependency. The goal is to help the right creators discover Nexkosmo, understand its real value, reach meaningful creative success, and choose to continue because the product genuinely helps them create, learn, collaborate, publish, and earn.

This is an operating framework, not a new Brain intelligence subsystem. ChatGPT may help with strategy, evidence review, positioning, experiment design, and analysis. Codex may implement approved instrumentation, landing pages, referral mechanics, analytics events, experiments, and product-growth features. The Director retains final authority over brand, positioning, launch, material spend, and consequential growth decisions.

## North-star marketing rule

**Product truth before promotion. Evidence before claims. Creator value before growth metrics.**

Marketing must never describe a planned capability as implemented, an estimate as an actual result, or an AI inference as customer evidence.

## Current positioning foundation

Nexkosmo's current product identity is a **Creative Production Operating System** centered on the relationship:

`Human Director <-> Nexkosmo AI Producer`

The human remains the Director. The AI Producer helps organize, plan, advise, coordinate, produce, explain, and improve while preserving human creative authority and creator ownership.

Marketing should communicate outcomes and experiences rather than presenting Nexkosmo as merely another generative model or image/video generator.

Core outcome arc:

`create -> collaborate -> produce -> publish -> grow -> earn`

Claims about any part of that arc must reflect actual product availability at the time of publication.

## Funnel model

Nexkosmo uses a full creator-success funnel:

`AWARENESS -> INTEREST -> REGISTRATION -> ACTIVATION -> VALUE -> RETENTION -> CREATION/PUBLISHING -> SHARE/REFERRAL -> REVENUE/CREATOR OUTCOME`

### Awareness

Help relevant creators discover Nexkosmo through useful, truthful content and communities.

Potential channels to test include creator communities, filmmaking communities, animation/game communities, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Discord, X, search, creator partnerships, events, educational content, and earned media.

Channel selection is experimental. No channel is canonical merely because it is listed here.

### Interest

Explain the product through concrete creator outcomes and demonstrations. Prefer showing a believable end-to-end experience over generic claims such as "AI-powered filmmaking platform."

### Registration

Registration is not success. Track it, but do not optimize the product solely for account creation.

### Activation

Activation is the first point where a creator experiences meaningful Nexkosmo value. The exact activation event is currently `UNKNOWN` and must be learned from evidence.

Candidate activation signals to test include:

- first project created;
- first meaningful Director/AI Producer interaction;
- first approved creative direction;
- first reusable canonical character/asset;
- first scene or production-ready artifact.

Do not lock an activation metric until user evidence supports it.

### Value and retention

Retention should come from continued creator value, not manipulative engagement loops. Re-engagement should be useful, relevant, controllable, and proportionate.

### Share and referral

The strongest long-term growth loop should come from creators making work worth sharing.

Potential loop:

`creator makes valuable work -> creator chooses to share -> audience discovers Nexkosmo attribution/project surface -> interested audience explores -> new creator starts -> new work is created`

Referral credits and incentives may strengthen this loop when implemented, but incentives must not create deceptive endorsements or obscure material relationships.

### Revenue and creator outcomes

Growth quality includes sustainable Nexkosmo economics and creator outcomes. Revenue must not be optimized in ways that degrade creator agency, ownership, trust, or product value.

## Marketing measurement model

Instrument the funnel before scaling spend. Metrics should use documented definitions, event sources, time windows, and data-quality status.

Priority measures, when implementation exists:

- qualified visitors;
- registrations;
- activation rate;
- activation time;
- retained active creators;
- project creation and meaningful project progression;
- creator output completion/publishing where applicable;
- share rate;
- referral conversion;
- free-to-paid conversion;
- customer acquisition cost (CAC);
- average revenue per paying creator;
- gross margin/contribution after compute where measurable;
- churn;
- lifetime value (LTV) only when enough history exists to estimate responsibly;
- creator-reported satisfaction/value;
- creator earnings/opportunities when marketplace/economy features exist;
- reasons for abandonment/churn.

Never invent a current value for a metric without telemetry. Missing metrics are `UNKNOWN`, not zero.

## Event and attribution principles

Growth instrumentation must be designed with privacy and security from the beginning.

- collect only data needed for a defined product/growth purpose;
- avoid putting secrets, private prompts, private scripts, private creative content, tokens, or unnecessary personal data into analytics events;
- use stable documented event names and schemas;
- distinguish anonymous/consented/authenticated identity appropriately;
- preserve workspace/project isolation;
- document attribution windows and limitations;
- do not silently combine unrelated data sources to infer sensitive traits;
- treat marketing analytics as evidence, not canonical truth about a person.

Security/privacy requirements in `docs/SECURE_DEVELOPMENT_PROTOCOL.md` apply to growth systems as well.

## Experiment protocol

Growth changes should be treated as falsifiable experiments rather than beliefs.

Each material experiment records:

- experiment ID;
- hypothesis;
- audience/segment;
- user problem or behavior being addressed;
- intervention/variant;
- primary metric;
- guardrail metrics;
- minimum observation window or stopping rule where practical;
- expected cost/exposure;
- data source;
- result;
- confidence/limitations;
- decision: keep, revise, reject, or continue gathering evidence.

Experiments must not be described as successful solely because one metric moved if guardrails, retention, economics, trust, or creator outcomes worsened materially.

## Growth guardrails

Nexkosmo must not use dark patterns or manipulative growth tactics.

Prohibited or strongly restricted practices include:

- fake scarcity/countdowns;
- fabricated testimonials, reviews, users, usage numbers, waitlists, revenue, creator earnings, or endorsements;
- misleading before/after demonstrations;
- undisclosed paid endorsements or incentives where disclosure is required;
- difficult cancellation designed to trap users;
- deceptive opt-in/opt-out controls;
- spam or unauthorized messaging;
- manipulative notifications designed primarily to induce anxiety/compulsion;
- false urgency;
- presenting planned features as available;
- hiding material limitations, costs, or conditions;
- automatically publishing a creator's private work for marketing;
- using private creator assets in promotional material without appropriate permission/rights.

The creator owns their work and chooses what becomes public, subject to applicable agreements and law.

## Claims assurance

Before a material public claim, classify it:

- `VERIFIED PRODUCT FACT`: demonstrated by current product/repository/runtime evidence;
- `VERIFIED BUSINESS FACT`: demonstrated by authoritative analytics/financial evidence;
- `CUSTOMER EVIDENCE`: supported by attributable research/testimonial data with appropriate consent;
- `ESTIMATE`: clearly labeled forward-looking calculation;
- `ASPIRATION`: future direction clearly stated as future;
- `UNKNOWN`: insufficient evidence; do not convert into a marketing claim.

Examples:

- "Nexkosmo supports X today" requires current implementation evidence.
- "Creators save 50%" requires defensible measured evidence, not intuition.
- "Coming soon" requires a genuine approved roadmap basis and must not imply current availability.

## Launch stages

Marketing scale follows product evidence.

### Stage 0 - Foundation

Current focus:

- positioning foundation;
- audience hypotheses;
- event/metric contracts;
- landing/demo message architecture;
- creator research plan;
- early-access criteria;
- launch asset requirements.

Avoid material paid acquisition.

### Stage 1 - Controlled creator validation

Use a small number of target creators to test product value and activation. Capture qualitative and quantitative evidence. The goal is learning, not vanity growth.

### Stage 2 - Early access

Open carefully to more creators once the vertical product slice and support/observability are strong enough. Test share/referral loops and community acquisition.

### Stage 3 - Repeatable organic growth

Scale channels that produce activated/retained creators, not merely cheap traffic.

### Stage 4 - Paid acquisition

Scale paid acquisition only after activation, retention, conversion, unit economics, and attribution are credible enough to avoid buying churn.

### Stage 5 - Ecosystem growth

Marketplace, creator economy, distribution, partnerships, education, and compute-network growth follow only when their underlying products and governance exist.

- referral credits;
- public creator/project discovery where explicitly published;
- templates/examples/tutorials;
- collaborative invitation loops;
- launch/distribution assistance from the AI Producer;
- post-release audience and opportunity insights.

All are `PLANNED` until implemented and verified.

## AI Producer and creator marketing

The AI Producer may eventually help the creator market their own work after release, including trailers, posters, social cuts, release planning, localization, audience insight, sequel/spin-off opportunities, and distribution options.

This support must remain under creator direction. It must not auto-publish, misrepresent audience evidence, manufacture social proof, impersonate people, or use creator assets outside permitted scope.

## Roles

### Director

Final authority for brand identity, positioning, launch decisions, major campaigns, material marketing spend, and consequential policy.

### ChatGPT

Growth/marketing strategy and evidence-review support. Challenge unproven claims, vanity metrics, weak experiments, channel assumptions, misleading messaging, poor unit economics, and conflicts with creator agency.

ChatGPT does not promote its own strategy to canon without Director approval and must distinguish evidence from hypothesis.

### Codex

Implements approved growth capabilities such as analytics events, attribution plumbing, landing experiences, referral mechanics, experiments, dashboards, and product-led loops. Codex must follow engineering, security, privacy, and alignment rules and must not hard-code invented marketing metrics or fake social proof.

### CI/tests

Verify enforceable growth contracts such as required framework files, event schemas/tests, prohibited hard-coded claims where practical, and future analytics contract checks. CI does not decide marketing strategy.

### Brain

Brain remains the Nexkosmo intelligence architecture. It may consume validated market/product evidence through existing responsibilities when appropriate, but this framework does not create a parallel "Marketing Brain" or grant autonomous campaign authority.

## Marketing work completion contract

For a material marketing/growth change, report:

- target audience;
- user problem/outcome;
- claim classification and evidence;
- funnel stage affected;
- metric/event definitions;
- experiment hypothesis where applicable;
- privacy/security impact;
- expected spend/cost if any;
- implementation status;
- observed result vs estimate;
- unknowns and risks;
- Director approval required/obtained for consequential launch, brand, spend, or public-claim decisions.

## Immediate priority

Until the first credible end-to-end Nexkosmo vertical slice is demonstrated, prioritize marketing foundation and controlled creator learning over broad paid acquisition.

The immediate sequence is:

`positioning + audience hypotheses + measurement contracts -> first real vertical slice -> controlled creator validation -> learn/repair -> early access -> share/referral loop -> repeatable organic growth -> paid acquisition when economics are evidenced`
